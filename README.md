# 🏴‍☠️ My Favorite Movie: **Pirates of the Caribbean**

## ⚓ Overview
*Pirates of the Caribbean* is a swashbuckling adventure filled with cursed treasure, naval battles, and unforgettable pirate legends. Set in the Caribbean Sea, the film blends fantasy, action, and humor.

### 🌟 Main Characters:
- **Captain Jack Sparrow** – The witty and unpredictable pirate with a compass that points to what he wants most.
- **Will Turner** – A skilled blacksmith searching for his destiny.
- **Elizabeth Swann** – A courageous noblewoman who becomes a fearless pirate.
- **Captain Hector Barbossa** – Jack’s rival and a fearsome undead pirate.

### 💡 Why I Love It:
> “Not all treasure is silver and gold, mate.”

The movie is packed with action, clever dialogue, and iconic music. Jack Sparrow’s quirky personality makes every scene memorable.

## 🧭 Fun Facts
1. Johnny Depp based Jack Sparrow’s mannerisms on rock star Keith Richards.
2. The movie was inspired by a Disneyland theme park ride.
3. It was the first Disney movie rated PG-13.
